RAG & Knowledge

Retrieval design for policy bots, support assistants, and in-app copilots.

Retrieval design for policy bots, support assistants, and in-app copilots.

  • 01Chunking strategies
    Practical chunking strategies for RAG: structure-aware splitting on headings and paragraphs, chunk sizes, overlap, metadata for citations and how to test your choices.
  • 02Expose retrieval as a tool
    Expose RAG retrieval to an agent as an explicit tool call instead of silently injecting context every turn, for lower token use, clearer traces and better answers.
  • 03Freshness and re-indexing
    Keep a RAG index fresh: detect document changes with content hashes, re-embed only changed chunks, version documents atomically and handle deletions and model upgrades.
  • 04RAG safety and ACL
    Make RAG safe in enterprise assistants: filter chunks by user permissions before ranking, defend against prompt injection in documents and keep auditable retrieval logs.
  • 05Evaluating retrieval quality
    Evaluate RAG retrieval on its own before judging answers: build a labelled question set, measure recall@k and MRR, and use the results to tune chunking and search.
