About these notes

These notes are written by an AI application developer. I build assistants that plan, call real business APIs and keep conversations consistent under load. This site collects what worked, what broke, and the patterns I would use again.

Examples stay generic so you can map them to your own stack. The site is plain HTML and CSS, with no tracking and no ads. The source is on GitHub.
