About me

I'm Shubham Mishra. I have built web products with Ruby on Rails since 2017, shipped iOS apps in Swift from 2017 to 2020, and spent four years on React before coming back to it in 2026. Since March 2026 I have been building agentic AI systems, and AI automation is where most of my curiosity goes now.

This site collects what worked, what broke, and the patterns I would use again. It is plain HTML and CSS, with no ads and no tracking.
