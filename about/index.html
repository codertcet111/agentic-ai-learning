What I work on now

I lead engineering teams and, since March 2026, build agentic AI systems: assistants that plan, call real business APIs on a user's behalf, pause for confirmation and keep conversations consistent under load. Years of Rails work shape how I approach it: business rules belong in the domain services, the agent orchestrates, and every write needs a clear owner.

I am especially interested in AI automation: taking repetitive, multi-step work that people do across several tools and handing it to agents that are observable, testable and safe to run.
