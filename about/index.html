What I work on now

I lead backend and frontend teams on Jisr's Ruby on Rails and React products. Since March 2026 I am also a hands-on member of our agentic AI team, where we learn and build together: agents that call real business APIs, a Text-to-SQL engine for our ATS, and an AI pipeline that triages production errors and opens fix branches. Years of Rails work shape how I approach it: business rules belong in the domain services, the agent orchestrates, and every write needs a clear owner.

I am especially interested in AI automation: handing repetitive, multi-step work to agents that are observable, testable and safe to run.
