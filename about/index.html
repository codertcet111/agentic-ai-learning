What I work on now

Since March 2026 my focus has been agentic AI: assistants that plan, call real business APIs on a user's behalf, pause for confirmation and keep conversations consistent under load. Years of Rails work shape how I approach it: business rules belong in the domain services, the agent orchestrates, and every write needs a clear owner.

I am especially interested in AI automation: taking repetitive, multi-step work that people do across several tools and handing it to agents that are observable, testable and safe to run.

About this site

Agentic Field Notes is where I write down patterns from building agent backends: the 20-step path from first boundary to golden tests, and deeper notes on graph orchestration, MongoDB patterns, retrieval, evaluation and tracing with Langfuse. Examples are generic on purpose, so you can map them onto your own domain.

The site is plain HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript, hosted free on GitHub Pages. Your reading progress is stored in a cookie in your own browser and is never sent anywhere. The source is on GitHub.
